Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

View Tracker is a personal utility for accessing and reviewing video information and statistics associated with an authorized account.

Information Accessed

When authorized by the user, View Tracker may access basic account information and public video information made available through the applicable API permissions.

Purpose of Access

Information is accessed only for personal video statistics analysis and record keeping.

Data Sharing

View Tracker does not sell personal information or use authorized account information for advertising.

Authorization

Access to account information is subject to user authorization and the permissions granted through the applicable third-party service.

Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be submitted through the project repository.
